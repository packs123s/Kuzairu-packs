Academic Programs

Comprehensive O-Level & A-Level Curriculum

Tailored subject combinations preparing students for university education and future careers.

O

Ordinary Level (S.1 - S.4)

Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE)

Following the NCDC competence-based lower secondary curriculum, developing practical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills.

Core Subjects Offered:

Mathematics English Language Physics & Chemistry Biology & Agriculture Geography & History Computer Studies / ICT
A

Advanced Level (S.5 - S.6)

Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE)

Specialized combinations in Sciences and Arts designed to maximize entry into university programs.

Popular Combinations:

Science Streams: PCM/ICT, BCM/Sub Math, PCB/Sub Math, MEG
Arts & Humanities: HEL/Sub Math, HEG/ICT, DEG/ICT, LED/ICT
